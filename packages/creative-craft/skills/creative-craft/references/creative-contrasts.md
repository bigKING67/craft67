# Creative contrasts

Read this when ideas remain generic, routes feel interchangeable, or comparing
executions needs a concrete example. These eight original fictional teaching
cases are editorial examples, not measured winners or approved brand work.
Case 8 identifies a headline accepted by the user during iterative development;
the other candidates remain editorial proposals.
“Candidate” means a reasoned alternative to test, not proven superiority.
Transfer the decision, not the headline, style, or situation. Do not load this
reference for every task or reproduce these comparisons in every answer.

## 1. Concept: give a feature a situation

**Facts and task:** A carry-on opens from the front while standing upright.
Communicate its usefulness in a cramped hotel room; no comparative size or time
savings are verified.

- Ordinary: “小空间，大从容。” A suitcase sits in a tidy hotel room.
- Candidate: “开箱，不必给箱子再开一间房。” A single frame shows the upright
  case open beside the bed while a traveler can still pass through the room.
- Novel but off-task: The suitcase unfolds into a hotel suite. This turns a
  real opening mechanism into an invented capacity or transformation claim.

**Decision:** The candidate makes floor space a recognizable travel problem and
ties the image to the opening mechanism. Stage a physically feasible clearance;
the copy must not imply that any hotel layout or all contents work equally well.
For a sober technical audience, a plain demonstration may outperform the joke.

## 2. Concept: turn a social tension into an action

**Facts and task:** A museum offers an evening session in which visitors may
sketch selected exhibits using paper and pencils. Invite hesitant beginners;
no visitor research or learning outcome is supplied.

- Ordinary: “释放艺术天赋，遇见更好的自己。”
- Candidate: “今晚，不必画得像。” The invitation pairs an exhibit silhouette
  with several visibly different, original visitor-style sketches and a clear
  invitation to the evening session.
- Novel but off-task: A masterpiece mocks every imperfect sketch. The comic
  device intensifies the very judgment the invitation proposes to relax.

**Decision:** Permission to try becomes the idea, rather than a promise of
transformation. Beginner anxiety is a working hypothesis, not a research claim.
Use original drawings; do not imply that pictured submissions are real visitor
testimonials. If the actual session teaches precise copying, this premise fails.

## 3. Copy: let a supported action replace praise

**Facts and task:** A notebook has perforated pages that can be detached. Write
a concise line for a page showing a detachable shopping list. No strength,
sustainability, or productivity evidence is supplied.

- Ordinary: “记录灵感，开启高效生活。”
- Candidate: “这一页，跟你出门。” Body: “沿虚线撕下购物清单，带去下一站。”
- Novel but off-task: “撕掉拖延，效率翻倍。” A detachable page does not establish
  a psychological effect or a numerical productivity gain.

**Decision:** The headline gives a physical feature a small human purpose; the
body explains the mechanism. It fits a shopping-list visual, not every notebook
use. If the product has no perforation, discard the execution rather than soften
the claim with adjectives.

## 4. Copy: use restraint without erasing the product

**Facts and task:** A fragrance has verified iris, paper, and cedar notes.
Write a sensory product paragraph. Order, longevity, and provenance are unknown.

- Ordinary: “高级木质香，诠释独一无二的优雅魅力。”
- Candidate: “鸢尾、纸张与雪松。想起翻开一本旧书的午后，书签还停在上次
  那一页。”
- Novel but off-task: “百年藏书提取物，三秒舒缓焦虑。” This invents an ingredient,
  origin, timing, and health effect.

**Decision:** The candidate locates the supplied notes in an imagined scene;
the book belongs to that recollection, not an ingredient or a promised effect
of wearing the fragrance. If that distinction is unclear in the placement,
use the plainer “鸢尾、纸张与雪松，让人联想到旧书与午后”。
Do not force literary nostalgia onto a brand or audience that does not want it.

## 5. Static visual: make relationships carry the message

**Facts and task:** A fictional tableware set contains three nesting bowls.
Develop one key visual about nesting; capacity and break resistance are unknown.

- Ordinary: Three bowls on a beige plinth with “简约生活，美好之选”。
- Candidate: An overhead frame shows the bowls nested, with all three rims
  visible and aligned beside a single folded napkin. Copy: “三只碗，一处落脚。”
- Novel but off-task: Bowls levitate inside a tiny floating house. The spectacle
  hides whether the bowls actually nest and introduces an unrelated story.

**Decision:** Visible geometry carries the claim; the napkin provides a familiar
  scale cue without a numerical space-saving promise. Inspect a real render for
  three distinct rims and correct product shapes before judging success. A
  different view may be necessary if overhead hides this product's nesting.

## 6. Motion: make time reveal the product relationship

**Facts and task:** A reading lamp has a pivoting head. Propose a six-second
  silent social video; brightness, eye-health, and battery claims are unknown.

- Ordinary: Beauty shots of the lamp, a book, and a relaxed reader, then a logo.
- Candidate: One locked shot. In the opening two seconds, a reader moves the
  book out of the lit area. In the next two, a hand pivots the lamp head toward
  it. Hold the lit page and “光，跟着这一页。” for the final two seconds.
- Novel but off-task: Light autonomously chases a book around the room. It
  implies tracking technology absent from the facts.

**Decision:** The change over time demonstrates adjustment without narration.
  The manual action must be visible, physically possible, and readable at the
  intended crop. These beats are a proposed timing plan, not verified footage.
  If the real mechanism needs longer, simplify or retime rather than speed up
  implausible movement.

## 7. Consumer copy: directness is a starting point, not the payoff

**Facts and task:** “晴坡” is a sugar-free carbonated drink with grapefruit
flavor, fine bubbles, and a 300 mL pack. Write a paragraph beside the bottle
that makes someone want to taste it. Juice content, sweeteners, and functional
benefits are unspecified.

- Ordinary: “给平常的一天，加一点轻快。像风吹过晴天的小山坡。” The scenery
  comes from the name; it says little about choosing or drinking this product.
- Fact-led candidate: “想喝点有味道的，糖就算了。晴坡无糖气泡饮，柚子风味，
  细密气泡，300毫升。” This proposes a recognizable choice, but that motivation
  is an audience hypothesis. The line remains usable by many sugar-free drinks;
  clearer wording alone does not establish appetite appeal or distinctiveness.
- Clever but wrong placement: “球队争不争气，这杯都有气。” Carbonation gives the
  wordplay a product connection, but a football occasion was not in this brief.
  A campaign headline cannot silently replace the requested product paragraph.

**Decision:** Judge the original job first: does the paragraph make the flavor
and drinking experience appealing, or merely list correct facts? The fact-led
candidate is a direction to revise, not a finished answer justified by its
contrast with weaker prose. Do not invent sweetness, bitterness, juice content,
or a tasting sequence to manufacture sensory specificity.

If football campaign exploration is separately in scope, the third line can
be tested as dialogue after a missed goal while a friend pours the drink.
Visible bubbles make the connection readable. Still ask whether the exchange
sounds natural, creates any desire to drink, and leaves the brand memorable.
It fits the carbonated-drink category more strongly than this particular brand;
showing the bottle supports identification but does not prove brand recall.
A scripted laugh or proposed fifteen-second timing is not audience or footage
evidence. Keep the route provisional until evaluated for its intended use.

## 8. Copy: connect a desired activity to a product action

**Facts and task:** “下班以后” is a fictional backpack with an independently
zipped bottom shoe compartment, separated from the main compartment by a fixed
divider. Invite people who bring shoes to exercise after work. Water, odor,
capacity, weight, and shoe-size performance are unknown. During development,
the user clarified that the invitation should encourage exercise and a break
from work, cover activities beyond ball sports, and evoke shoes in the headline.

- Obscure metaphor: “楼下，等你下班。” The author interpreted the lower
  compartment as a downstairs room. The user could not understand the headline
  or its product connection; explaining the metaphor did not rescue it.
- Feature explanation: “下班去运动，鞋有单独一仓。” It names a compartment
  without saying why the person wants to bring or change shoes.
- Practical concern: “换下来的鞋，不想和衣服混着放。” It supplies a storage
  reason, but the user rejected the wording and asked why changing shoes mattered.
- Useful but narrow: “下班，球场见。” The user liked the invitation but wanted
  it to include gyms and other exercise destinations.
- Broad but weakly tied to the product: “下班，去出汗。” The user liked the
  direction but wanted shoes expressed or implied in the headline.
- Accepted headline: **“下班，换双鞋去出汗。”**

**Supporting execution:** “运动鞋放独立鞋仓，与主仓分开放。” Show a person
changing shoes beside the open bottom compartment before exercise, using real
product geometry and a feasible load. The headline gives changing shoes a desired
purpose; the image and supporting copy explain how the bag participates. It does
not promise that carrying the bag improves exercise habits or removes work stress.

**Decision:** Keep the invitation the user liked and add the missing product
action, rather than returning to storage instructions or listing every feature.
The learning is to connect a desired activity with a credible product role, not
to make every headline follow “occasion + action + outcome” or mention shoes.
Some products serve pleasure or identity without solving an inconvenience.

**Evidence boundary:** The user accepted this headline after multiple rounds of
collaborative editing. This was not an untouched model output, a blind-test win,
a measured audience response, or public-release approval. The original backpack
comparison was rejected and remains so. This task is now teaching/diagnostic
material and cannot serve as an unseen test for a later revision.
