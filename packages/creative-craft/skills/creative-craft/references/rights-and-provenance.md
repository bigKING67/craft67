# Rights and provenance

Creative control includes knowing what may legally and ethically be used.

This reference is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Source checks

For every meaningful source, record:

- creator or source;
- owner;
- license or permission;
- likeness and voice consent;
- trademark or packaging permission;
- music, sound, stock, font, and location rights;
- allowed modification;
- channels, territories, duration, and expiration;
- disclosure or attribution requirements;
- confidential or embargo status.

## Sensitive identity use

Do not create or edit media that impersonates a real person without appropriate
consent and provider-compliant identity handling. Record whether a reference is:

- fictional;
- AI-generated;
- owned talent;
- licensed stock;
- public figure used within allowed policy and law;
- private person with explicit consent;
- unresolved.

A user-supplied image is not automatically proof of authorization.

## Product and brand fidelity

When a real product or brand is supplied:

- preserve geometry, label, logo, color, and required legal copy when fidelity
  is part of the task;
- do not invent certifications, awards, ingredients, performance claims, or
  endorsements;
- identify whether logo/trademark use is owned, licensed, comparative, or
  unresolved.

## Audio

Record rights separately for:

- dialogue/voice;
- voice likeness or cloning;
- music composition;
- master recording;
- sound effects;
- ambience;
- generated audio;
- replacement plan for temporary tracks.

## Provenance record

Recommended fields:

- stable asset ID;
- source URI or controlled location;
- SHA-256 checksum;
- MIME type;
- creator;
- rights status;
- consent status;
- allowed use;
- expiration;
- reference role;
- parent assets;
- provider/model;
- prompt or prompt hash;
- transformations;
- output checksum;
- disclosure state.

## Claims

A visually compelling execution does not validate a claim. Connect every
regulated, health, performance, environmental, financial, or comparative claim
to approved evidence and required qualification.

## Delivery gate

Block final delivery when:

- required consent is unresolved;
- the source license forbids the use or modification;
- a product claim lacks approved substantiation;
- a temporary track or placeholder asset remains;
- required attribution or disclosure is missing.

Exploration may continue with clearly marked placeholders when appropriate, but
the output must not be presented as cleared for publication.
