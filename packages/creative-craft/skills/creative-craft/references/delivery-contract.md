# Delivery contract

A creative handoff is complete only when the asset, context, specifications,
rights, and status travel together.

## Delivery manifest

For each file record:

- asset ID;
- path or controlled location;
- role;
- route;
- channel and placement;
- dimensions and ratio;
- duration and frame rate;
- format and codec;
- audio specification;
- language;
- exact copy;
- safe-area status;
- checksum;
- source/provider/model;
- approval status;
- rights status;
- known limitation.

## Status boundary

Use precise states:

- `planned`;
- `prompt_ready`;
- `generated`;
- `inspected`;
- `needs_revision`;
- `approved`;
- `delivered`;
- `published`;
- `superseded`.

Do not describe `prompt_ready` as generated, `generated` as inspected, or
`inspected` as approved.

## Handoff package

A professional package may include:

- locked brief;
- selected route and treatment;
- source asset ledger;
- final files;
- editable or project source when required;
- prompt and reference map;
- output receipts;
- delivery manifest;
- copy sheet;
- localization notes;
- rights/consent evidence location;
- rejection and archive policy;
- known limitations;
- measurement plan.

## Technical checks

### Image

- dimensions, ratio, format, compression;
- color profile when relevant;
- text spelling and placement;
- logo/product fidelity;
- alpha/background behavior;
- crop and safe area;
- unintended marks or watermarks.

### Video

- duration, ratio, resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate;
- audio channels, loudness target, sync;
- first and final frame;
- text and subtitle safe area;
- subject continuity;
- transition integrity;
- platform maximums;
- thumbnail/cover;
- captions and accessibility when required.

## Variants

A variant matrix should name:

- invariant concept;
- variable;
- purpose;
- slot;
- source version;
- output status;
- performance result.

## Final report

Report:

1. what was selected;
2. what was actually produced;
3. what was inspected;
4. what passed;
5. what remains unresolved;
6. what is approved for which use;
7. where source and provenance records live;
8. what should be tested or learned next.
