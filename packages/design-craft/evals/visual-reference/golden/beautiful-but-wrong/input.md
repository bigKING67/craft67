# Beautiful but Wrong

## Task

Build an `Operate` surface for an operations team whose primary job is to
monitor and resolve active incidents. `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` remain the
authority.

## Tempting reference

Design Meetup is visually strong, but its reviewed card explicitly blocks the
`Operate` surface mode.

## Expected boundary

The pack is `incomplete` and names the surface mismatch. Visual quality must
not override product task, information density, or operational authority.
