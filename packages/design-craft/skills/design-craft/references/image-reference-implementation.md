# Implementing a design image

Use for a supplied or selected image that the user wants implemented. This is
an internal design-craft capability, not a dependency on another design Skill.
For ordinary UI work without an image deliverable, do not introduce generation.

## Establish what the image controls

The selected image controls the agreed visual direction, subject to explicit
user constraints and project authority. It does not silently replace approved
copy, brand facts, product behavior, or an existing design system. Distinguish
faithful reconstruction from taking inspiration; preserve asset rights in both.

Inspect the original at adequate resolution. Use a labeled crop for a detail,
retaining its relationship to the full composition. Do not regenerate an
approved image to discover its missing detail: a new generation is a proposal,
not evidence of the original. Ask only when an unresolved difference changes
scope, acceptance, cost, or the intended design.

Direct implementation does not require a discovery catalog or Reference Pack.
When importing third-party examples, curating reusable patterns, or using a
Shadow Lab, follow `reference-workflow.md` and its evidence/rights contracts.

## Recover the decisions that affect fidelity

- Content: section order, readable text, navigation, CTA hierarchy, and required copy.
- Geometry: container proportions, columns, gutters, spacing cadence, padding,
  media ratios, overlaps, and alignment anchors.
- Typography: display/body relationships, weight, line height, tracking,
  wrapping, and text/media balance. A screenshot alone does not identify a font.
- Components: fill/outline, radius relationships, dividers, icon placement,
  and recurring primary/secondary treatments.
- Color and media: background/surface/ink/accent roles, crop, grading, and depth.

Measure decisive proportions where practical and label estimates. An image
cannot prove hidden states, interactions, responsive layouts, or accessibility.
Generated logos, metrics, testimonials, and names are not verified facts;
approved copy and supplied assets outrank garbled or invented concept content.

## Implement and compare

Map repeated properties to project tokens and components. Preserve hierarchy,
section sequence, distinctive asymmetry, imagery, and reading order instead of
flattening the reference into generic cards. Keep approved copy intact rather
than shortening it to force a headline line count. Control image aspect ratio
and object positioning where a crop carries the composition.

At narrow widths, adapt geometry while preserving priority and usable controls.
Do not hide overflow to conceal broken layout or infer a functioning interaction
from a painted control. Implement relevant focus, loading, error, and responsive
states from the product requirements.

Compare the implemented surface at a compatible viewport and capture scale.
Resolve major geometry, type, color, and media mismatches before decoration.
Use `comp-fidelity.md` only for requested controlled measurements or sealed
rendition; a pixel metric or screenshot alone is not visual acceptance. The
normal `validation-contract.md` and `system-review.md` govern UI closeout.
Report material deviations and unverified behavior, without repeating checks
whose inputs and relevant evidence have not changed.
