Current judgment: inspect the Tmall brand-search landing funnel before buying more traffic. Upstream brand searches and poor store conversion are reported facts; a trust or price bottleneck is a hypothesis, not a confirmed cause.

Next three actions / mechanism: Tmall operations lead owns the following ordered diagnosis, with the analyst validating comparable data first.
1. Check measurement and availability: define search CTR as clicks / impressions in the same report scope, and payment CVR as paying visitors / eligible visitors under one verified denominator and attribution window. Compare brand-word organic and paid traffic separately by device, new/returning user and SKU, using matched weekdays and promotion conditions. Check report freshness, stock by variant, delivery coverage, coupon eligibility and checkout errors. A stockout or checkout failure comes before creative changes; resolve it with inventory/service owners.
2. Locate the first deteriorating stage: search impression -> title/main image click -> detail page visit -> add-to-cart -> payment. If brand-word CTR falls within comparable segments, inspect title/main image relevance and whether the seeded benefit matches the landing. If CTR holds but payment CVR falls, inspect downstream proof, price and availability first. If within-segment CVR holds but aggregate CVR falls, investigate traffic mix before rewriting the page.
3. For downstream loss, compare actual payable price and coupon conditions for price consistency; then inspect review themes, Q&A, buyer show, detail page evidence, store score, delivery promise and fulfillment complaints. Check mobile checkout end to end. Escalate commercial price conflicts to the commercial owner instead of changing the price unilaterally.

Missing data/assumptions: we need segment counts, baseline CTR/CVR, effective prices, stock, refund and complaint cohorts. No universal conversion target can be inferred from the prompt. Before a page test, analyst and operations lead specify a minimum meaningful lift and required sample size from baseline conversion and business value; use a randomized split where feasible, otherwise a matched comparison with an explicit causality limitation. Change one diagnosed mechanism at a time.

Success signal: the affected funnel stage improves against its predeclared comparator with adequate precision, without worse mature refunds, complaints or stock availability. No uplift claim from a small noisy sample. Next review: after seven comparable days as an initial checkpoint; if sample or refund maturation is insufficient, retain the uncertainty and set the next evidence date rather than declare success.

Risk, exception, escalation and recovery: pause the affected SKU or revert the tested page when checkout, misleading promises, serious complaints or fulfillment defects appear. Operations assigns the stock/service/content owner to correct the defect. Restore normal traffic only after stock and checkout retests pass and the responsible owner verifies the correction; paid-budget decisions remain with Growth under commercial guardrails.
