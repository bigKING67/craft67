Price ladder: official launch price, daily price, member benefit, outfit bundle price, seasonal campaign price, clearance price for old season, minimum profit price, forbidden floor price.

Content quality gate: every asset must match the approved size/specification sheet and show material, fit, color, and use limits without hiding exceptions. Rights/usage boundary: record model, creator, music, image, channel, territory, and usage term before reuse. Refresh rule: retire or update any asset when the SKU, size guidance, season, rights term, or measured return reason changes.

Fulfillment/refund/after-sale: inventory by size/color, return reason tagging, exchange CS script, defective stitching/zipper/fabric watchlist.

Stop rule: stop channel/SKU if return rate or size complaints exceed plan. Scale rule: scale only when payment CVR, return, buyer show quality, and seasonal inventory are stable.

Review cadence: weekly size-color inventory and return review; monthly seasonal markdown governance.

Mechanism: use size/fit evidence and reason-coded returns to gate channel and SKU expansion. Owner: operations lead with merchandising, content, service, and warehouse owners. Success signal: payment CVR improves while size returns and aging inventory remain below threshold.

Exception and recovery: isolate affected size-color inventory, correct content or size guidance, and reopen only after the defect is verified. Escalation: quality, safety, or repeated fit issues go to the accountable product owner before more traffic is released.
